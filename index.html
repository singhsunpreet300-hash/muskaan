About the Language

What is "Theth" Punjabi?

ਠੇਠ (Theth) means pure or genuine. Theth Punjabi refers to the unadulterated, classical form of the language — rich with words that have survived from ancient Indo-Aryan roots, preserved in Guru Granth Sahib Ji, in the poetry of Bulla Shah and Waris Shah, and in the mouths of elders in the villages of Majha, Malwa, and Doaba.

Unlike modern colloquial Punjabi — which borrows heavily from Urdu, Hindi, and English — Theth Punjabi carries the full flavour of the culture: its agricultural metaphors, its devotional vocabulary, its earthy humour.

"ਬੋਲੀ ਉਹ ਜੋ ਦਿਲ ਛੂਹੇ।" The language that touches the heart.
